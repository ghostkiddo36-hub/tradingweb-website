Standards

Platform rules

A professional workspace needs clear boundaries for users and administrators.

01 · Respect

No harassment, threats, discrimination or targeted abuse.

02 · No fraud

No scams, impersonation, deceptive claims or attempts to steal credentials.

03 · Privacy

Do not publish private information or another person's personal data.

04 · Legitimate use

No malicious code, phishing, illegal activity or deliberate platform abuse.

05 · Trading risk

Trading carries financial risk. Users make their own financial decisions.

06 · Administration

The owner may manage platform content, campaigns, applications and moderation subject to applicable law.
